### F-053

**Rebuild a bando as a point cloud from a flight's video, placed by its blackbox and GPS**

| Signal | Priority | Status | Effort |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| 🔴 | 🔹 P3 | Open | 🔴 Large |

🧑 **→ Human action required: Decision**<br />Choose survey pass and import tool, in-app reconstruction, or stop: [options and results](../../../tools/prototypes/video-cloud/README.md#options-for-the-owner).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Issue** | Measured bandos are modelled by hand from photos and top views; a flight's video already holds the geometry, and its blackbox and GPS hold the camera's attitude, scale and place. |
| **Reproduce** | Take one flight with video, blackbox log and GPS (the demo flight); make a point cloud from the video in each approach of the trial and lay it over the bando in the editor. |
| **Check** | A trial note names, per approach, whether it runs on a Mac without CUDA, its time per minute of video, and whether walls and roof lines can be traced within about 0.5 m; the owner then decides whether to build it. |

<details>
<summary>Next action, estimates and PR history</summary>

**Next action / closure evidence:** Trial done 2026-11-24 on the demo flight (freestyle, GPS at 1°N 1°E); prototypes, commands, results table and the three options: [tools/prototypes/video-cloud](../../../tools/prototypes/video-cloud/README.md). Route (b), log poses and Depth Anything V2 small on WebGPU ([#288](https://github.com/voidbrain/openbando/pull/288)): 31 s for 102 s of video, but not traceable; the per-frame error is in the depth and the GPS, not a fixed lens or pose offset. Route (a), COLMAP 4.2 on the CPU (Homebrew, installed by the owner), 6 frames a second, [#289](https://github.com/voidbrain/openbando/pull/289): 496 of 614 frames in 17 pieces, each placed by the log; one wall spreads 0.2–0.6 m and corners are square within 1–3°, but neighbouring pieces disagree by 2.2–3.0 m (two runs) (cameras vs log: median 1.5 m), because flips and sky frames break the chain of matches. The log as `pose_prior_mapper` priors did not join them; a fisheye camera model verified no pair. lingbot-map (CUDA) and Brush were not run: Brush needs one connected model to be worth training. Waiting on the owner's decision; option 1 starts with one survey-style flight (slow pass or orbit, no flips, with blackbox and GPS). Privacy: a cloud of a private place never enters the repository, an export, a hidden bando or a test.

**Estimates:** Large remaining effort. Trial done; option 1 needs a survey flight, an import script and an editor layer; option 2 is larger. Re-estimated 2026-11-24; not acceptance evidence.
