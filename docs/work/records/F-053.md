| **Issue** | Measured bandos are modelled by hand from photos and top views; a flight's video already holds the geometry, and its blackbox and GPS hold the camera's attitude, scale and place. |

<details>
<summary>Next action, estimates and PR history</summary>

**Next action / closure evidence:** Requested 2026-11-24 by the owner, after asking whether [lingbot-map](https://github.com/robbyant/lingbot-map) (streaming 3D reconstruction from video, Apache-2.0) could serve OpenBando, and asking for our own TypeScript / WebAssembly version since there is no NVIDIA GPU here. lingbot-map itself needs PyTorch and CUDA (FlashInfer; an SDPA fallback exists, Apple's MPS untested), so it is at most a quality reference on a rented GPU. What OpenBando adds that general tools lack: the video is already synced to the log (`core/sync`), so every frame has the blackbox attitude (gyro, high rate) and a GPS position; the reconstruction needs no pose search from nothing, and scale and place come from the telemetry, not from guesses.<br />Trial, offline, results kept out of the repository: (a) the Mac-ready tools, [Brush](https://github.com/ArthurBrussee/brush) (WebGPU Gaussian splats, Apache-2.0, takes COLMAP data) and COLMAP's browser port, on frames of one flight; (b) a TypeScript prototype under `tools/prototypes/`: camera poses seeded from the synced log, per-frame depth from a small monocular model in the browser (Depth Anything, ONNX through transformers.js on WebGPU, WASM fallback), the depths scaled and fused in site metres into one point cloud and shown over the bando; (c) optionally lingbot-map on a rented GPU as the reference. Known risks: the FPV lens is wide or fisheye (needs a calibration), GPS is good to a few metres (the visual fit must refine position), motion blur and props in frame.<br />After the trial: a Decision for the owner (an in-app "trace from flight" layer, an offline import tool, or drop), recorded here with its options. Privacy: a point cloud of a private place shows it in detail; it never enters the repository, an export, a hidden bando or a test (AGENTS "Privacy"); test data stays neutral.

**Estimates:** Large remaining effort. Trial of three approaches with a prototype fusion step; no code yet. Estimated 2026-11-24; not acceptance evidence.
