### F-053

**Rebuild a bando as a point cloud from a flight's video, placed by its blackbox and GPS**

| Signal | Priority | Status | Effort |
|:---:|---|---|---|
| 🔴 | 🔹 P3 | Open | 🔴 Large |

🧑 **→ Human action required: Procedure**<br />Fly one survey pass with video, blackbox and GPS, and give the agent the folder: [survey flight steps](../../../tools/prototypes/video-cloud/README.md#survey-flight-the-owners-step).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Issue** | Measured bandos are modelled by hand from photos and top views; a flight's video already holds the geometry, and its blackbox and GPS hold the camera's attitude, scale and place. |
| **Reproduce** | Fly the [survey flight](../../../tools/prototypes/video-cloud/README.md#survey-flight-the-owners-step) over a bando, then run `colmap.sh` on its video and log (auto-sync, COLMAP, placement by the log, score). |
| **Check** | The survey flight gives one connected model placed by the log, walls spreading under about 0.5 m and parallel walls at the bando's measured sizes; only then an editor reference layer is built. |

<details>
<summary>Next action, estimates and PR history</summary>

**Next action / closure evidence:** Decided 2026-11-24 by the owner: option 1, survey pass then an import tool. `colmap.sh` now takes any video and log and syncs them with the app's own auto-sync (`sync.mjs`: −0.695 s, high confidence, on the demo; the app's `DEMO_OFFSET` is −0.69 s), [#290](https://github.com/voidbrain/openbando/pull/290). Next: the owner flies the survey pass; the agent runs `colmap.sh` on it and records the score here; if it meets the Check, plan the editor reference layer (privacy as below). Trial done 2026-11-24 on the demo flight (freestyle, GPS at 1°N 1°E); prototypes, commands, results table and the three options: [tools/prototypes/video-cloud](../../../tools/prototypes/video-cloud/README.md). Route (b), log poses and Depth Anything V2 small on WebGPU ([#288](https://github.com/voidbrain/openbando/pull/288)): 31 s for 102 s of video, but not traceable; the per-frame error is in the depth and the GPS, not a fixed lens or pose offset. Route (a), COLMAP 4.2 on the CPU (Homebrew, installed by the owner), 6 frames a second, [#289](https://github.com/voidbrain/openbando/pull/289): 496 of 614 frames in 17 pieces, each placed by the log; one wall spreads 0.2–0.6 m and corners are square within 1–3°, but neighbouring pieces disagree by 2.2–3.0 m (two runs) (cameras vs log: median 1.5 m), because flips and sky frames break the chain of matches. The log as `pose_prior_mapper` priors did not join them; a fisheye camera model verified no pair. lingbot-map (CUDA) and Brush were not run: Brush needs one connected model to be worth training. Privacy: a cloud of a private place never enters the repository, an export, a hidden bando or a test.

**Estimates:** Large remaining effort. Option 1 chosen; the survey run, then an import step and an editor layer remain. Re-estimated 2026-11-24; not acceptance evidence.
