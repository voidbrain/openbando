<summary>Next action, estimates and PR history</summary>

**Next action / closure evidence:** Route (b) tried 2026-11-24 on the demo flight (prototype and how to run it: `tools/prototypes/video-cloud/`, this PR; outputs kept outside the repository). Depth Anything V2 small on WebGPU in Chrome on an M3 Pro: 511 frames (5 a second over 102 s) in 31 s, about 60 ms a frame, so speed is no obstacle. Poses from the app's own code (`parseLog`, `prepareFlight`, `ReplayPose`, 25° uptilt, offset −0.69 s), each frame's relative depth scaled by fitting the ground under the drone, fused in 0.25 m cells. Result: not traceable. 208 of 511 frames fit the ground; about 30% of cells are seen by three or more frames; wall lines spread 0.3–0.9 m with few points; the top view is smeared ground fans, the 150 m Main store does not show, no parallel walls at its 55.2 m or the greenhouse's 20.3 m, only 2–3 m doubles of one wall. Lens (pinhole 90–120°, fisheye 120–180°), yaw (±25°) and tilt (10–40°) barely change agreement between frames, so the error varies per frame (the depth model's scale and shape, GPS jitter, sync): telemetry cannot replace matching frames to each other; it can give scale, north and place. Next: route (a), COLMAP's browser port for poses from the images, then Brush, scaled and placed by the log; our own TypeScript frame matching only if (a) shows the footage can be rebuilt.<br />Earlier plan: Requested 2026-11-24 by the owner, after asking whether [lingbot-map](https://github.com/robbyant/lingbot-map) (streaming 3D reconstruction from video, Apache-2.0) could serve OpenBando, and asking for our own TypeScript / WebAssembly version since there is no NVIDIA GPU here. lingbot-map itself needs PyTorch and CUDA (FlashInfer; an SDPA fallback exists, Apple's MPS untested), so it is at most a quality reference on a rented GPU. What OpenBando adds that general tools lack: the video is already synced to the log (`core/sync`), so every frame has the blackbox attitude (gyro, high rate) and a GPS position; the reconstruction needs no pose search from nothing, and scale and place come from the telemetry, not from guesses.<br />Trial, offline, results kept out of the repository: (a) the Mac-ready tools, [Brush](https://github.com/ArthurBrussee/brush) (WebGPU Gaussian splats, Apache-2.0, takes COLMAP data) and COLMAP's browser port, on frames of one flight; (b) a TypeScript prototype under `tools/prototypes/`: camera poses seeded from the synced log, per-frame depth from a small monocular model in the browser (Depth Anything, ONNX through transformers.js on WebGPU, WASM fallback), the depths scaled and fused in site metres into one point cloud and shown over the bando; (c) optionally lingbot-map on a rented GPU as the reference. Known risks: the FPV lens is wide or fisheye (needs a calibration), GPS is good to a few metres (the visual fit must refine position), motion blur and props in frame.<br />After the trial: a Decision for the owner (an in-app "trace from flight" layer, an offline import tool, or drop), recorded here with its options. Privacy: a point cloud of a private place shows it in detail; it never enters the repository, an export, a hidden bando or a test (AGENTS "Privacy"); test data stays neutral.
